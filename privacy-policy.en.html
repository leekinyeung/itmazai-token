2. Purposes and legal bases

We rely on the legal basis applicable to each processing activity. Depending on the activity, this may include processing necessary to enter into or perform a contract to which you are a party, to fulfill statutory duties or legal obligations, or your consent where required by law. We do not apply a single legal basis indiscriminately to every processing activity.

  • Providing the service: managing accounts, authenticating requests, forwarding model calls, delivering results and maintaining orders and service entitlements.
  • Security and compliance: identifying unusual access, troubleshooting failures, addressing abuse and complaints, and fulfilling applicable cybersecurity and record retention obligations.
  • Communication and support: sending verification codes, service changes, security alerts and transaction notices, and responding to your inquiries.
  • Optional features: processing information for account linking, referrals, saved preferences and other features you choose.

If we intend to use information for a new purpose incompatible with the original purpose, we will explain the change in advance and obtain renewed consent where required by law. Accepting this Policy does not provide blanket authorization for marketing messages, non-essential tracking or similar activities.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies, browser local storage or other necessary client-side storage mechanisms to maintain sign-in sessions, perform security verification and save language, theme, notification and interface preferences. The online model playground stores some conversation content and parameter settings in your current browser's local storage so that your working state can be restored.

Locally stored conversations remain within the device and browser environment you use. When you actually send a model request, the relevant conversation context is still transmitted to the platform and the corresponding external provider as part of the request. Clearing local conversations or website data does not automatically delete platform logs, task records or copies retained by third parties.

You can clear website data or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Clearing or restricting necessary storage may sign you out, remove saved preferences or prevent some features from working properly. After using a public or shared device, sign out and clear website data as appropriate. If we introduce non-essential analytics, advertising or tracking technologies, we will explain their purposes, providers and opt-out methods, and obtain consent in advance where required by law.

4. AI requests, outputs and logs

4.1 Request forwarding and task processing

To provide the AI service you select, the platform reads and processes request content, makes necessary format conversions according to the model and routing configuration, and sends the content and parameters relevant to completing that request to the corresponding external provider. Asynchronous tasks may involve storing task identifiers, status, necessary task data, generated outputs or output links. Files and external links in your inputs may also be accessed by the platform or external provider as required by the relevant API.

4.2 Usage logs and content retention

The platform may record account associations, models, call times, usage, charges, latency, status, request identifiers and error information in usage logs. Whether complete inputs or outputs are persistently retained depends on the service's actual content logging practices. Some task records, error messages, debug logs or support materials may also contain excerpts from content you submit.

Proposed content handling rule: ordinary API Inputs and Outputs are processed to complete the request rather than retained as a permanent conversation archive. Request bodies may temporarily use memory or disk buffering; normal request cleanup releases that buffer. Task data, result references and error or diagnostic excerpts may persist separately. Under the proposed schedule, task data and diagnostic excerpts are removed within 30 days after the task or investigation ends, subject to legal preservation. This is a target operating rule that must be checked against the deployed version and logging configuration. External retention is separate and may be longer.

You can manage the "Record IP Address" option for usage and error logs in your account security settings. This option only controls IP recording in those particular logs. It does not affect other IP processing necessary for sign-in sessions, security audits, network access or legal obligations. Disabling the option does not automatically delete existing records.

4.3 Training, staff access and processing rules

We process inputs and outputs to provide the service you request. If we intend to use that content separately for model training, advertising or another independent purpose, we will explain the specific purpose, scope and rules in advance and obtain additional consent where required by law. Staff handling troubleshooting or security incidents should access information only to the extent necessary and within their authorized permissions.

Processing may involve separate retention, security monitoring or model-improvement rules, depending on the feature and applicable arrangements. This Policy does not promise that every processing route has zero retention or excludes all content from training. Material processing conditions must be disclosed before the relevant activity, with any legally required consent obtained separately.

8. Security and incident response

We will take necessary technical and organizational measures appropriate to the types, purposes and risks of the information processed. These include access controls, identity verification, necessary protection in transit, credential protection, action auditing, and backup and security management measures appropriate to the actual deployment. Passwords should be verified and stored using one-way password hashing. API tokens, external keys and other credentials needed to authenticate or forward requests are subject to access restrictions and protection appropriate to their purposes.

Protect your account, tokens and verification information, enable multi-factor authentication or passkeys as appropriate, and promptly change passwords, revoke tokens or end unusual sessions if you suspect exposure. Before sending support materials, redact passwords, verification codes, recovery codes, complete keys and personal information unrelated to the issue.

If a personal information breach, alteration, loss or similar security incident occurs or may occur, we will take remedial measures and make reports and notifications required by applicable law, explaining the information involved, potential impacts, measures taken and protective steps you can take.

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

To the extent provided by applicable law, you may exercise the following rights:

  • Access and copies: view relevant information through account profile, token, session and log pages. Where log exports are enabled, you can export your own usage logs. You may request information unavailable through the interface through the request process in Section 13.
  • Correction and completion: update editable information in the relevant settings or contact us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: request deletion when the processing purpose has been fulfilled, a retention period has expired, consent has been withdrawn or another statutory condition applies. Browser-local records, platform data and external copies involve distinct processing scopes.
  • Withdrawal of consent and restriction: disable optional features, unlink accounts or contact us to withdraw relevant consent or request restricted processing. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully performed before withdrawal or necessary processing supported by another lawful basis.
  • Account deletion: use "Delete Account" on the account security page or contact us. Before deletion, you may export information, address outstanding transactions and save necessary records. Account deletion does not remove our legal retention and protection obligations.
  • Transfer and explanation: where the conditions under applicable law are met, request the transfer of your personal information or ask us to explain this Policy and related processing rules.

Use a service communication channel actually published or supplied to you by TaoDock, identifying the relevant account and the specific action requested. Section 13 explains the current request-channel status. We may conduct identity verification proportionate to the risk and collect only information necessary for that verification. We will not ask for your account password, complete API key or recovery codes.

Proposed response target: acknowledge a received request within three business days and provide a substantive response within 15 business days after necessary identity verification. A shorter mandatory deadline prevails; any permitted extension must be explained. These are proposed service commitments that require a functioning request channel. If a request cannot be fulfilled, explain the reason and available further steps. Statutory complaint and dispute remedies remain available.
