3. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies, browser local storage and similar mechanisms to maintain sessions, perform necessary security checks and remember language, theme and interface preferences. The model playground also stores some conversations and parameters in your current browser to restore your working state.

Local conversations remain in your device and browser environment. When you make a model request, relevant context is still sent to the platform and corresponding provider. You can use available clearing controls or browser settings to delete website data or restrict cookies, which may sign you out or remove local history. Clearing website data does not also delete server logs, task records or third-party copies.

If we introduce non-essential analytics, advertising or tracking, we will explain the purposes, providers and controls and obtain any consent required by law.

4. AI requests, results and logs

4.1 Request processing

The platform receives your request, performs any necessary format conversion according to the selected model, API and routing configuration, and sends the inputs, context, files and parameters needed to the relevant model or channel provider. External links you submit may be retrieved by the platform or provider. Do not submit private resources that should not be accessed or links containing credentials.

4.2 Content and usage records

Usage logs primarily record models, times, usage, charges, status and errors; they are distinct from full conversation history. Requests may use memory or temporary files. Asynchronous tasks may retain task data, results or result links. Errors, diagnostic logs and support materials may contain input or output excerpts. Ordinary API forwarding therefore does not mean that every processing stage has no content retention.

Account security settings let you manage IP recording in usage and error logs. Disabling it affects subsequent IP recording in those logs. It does not automatically erase previous records or prevent necessary IP processing for sessions, security audits or network access.

4.3 Training and human access

We process inputs and outputs to provide the service you request. Before separately using this content for model training, advertising or another independent purpose, we will explain the purpose and scope and obtain any required consent. Human access for troubleshooting, complaints or security incidents should be limited to authorized personnel and the necessary scope.

External model or channel providers may apply their own retention, safety monitoring and model improvement rules. Review the relevant conditions before submitting sensitive or confidential content. This Policy makes no universal zero-retention or no-training commitment for providers. Contact us first if you require specific processing conditions.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain personal information for the shortest period needed to provide services and meet applicable obligations. Different records serve different purposes:

  • Account information: supports the account and related services. After closure, only information necessary for legal obligations or unresolved matters is retained.
  • Requests, tasks and diagnostics: retained as needed for processing, result delivery, troubleshooting and necessary preservation. Temporary request processing is distinct from a permanent conversation archive.
  • Usage, security and transaction records: retained as needed for usage enquiries, reconciliation, security audits, disputes and legal requirements.
  • Support communications: retained for handling the matter and necessary follow-up.
  • Browser-local information: retained until cleared by you, removed by the feature or deleted by browser or device mechanisms.

When information is no longer needed and no retention obligation applies, we will delete or anonymize it. Information temporarily retained for legal obligations, unresolved disputes or technical limitations remains protected and subject to restricted use. Backup copies follow the relevant backup-management process. Account closure does not mean immediate physical erasure from every database, log, backup and third-party copy. A page's query window is not an automatic deletion period.

For specific retention arrangements or a deletion request, contact support@taodock.com. We will respond based on the record category, actual processing and applicable requirements.

8. How we protect information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to data categories and risks, including transport protection, authentication, access controls, credential protection and necessary auditing. Account passwords are verified using one-way hashes rather than plaintext verification records. API tokens and other credentials needed for verification or external connections receive access restrictions and protection appropriate to their use.

Protect your passwords, API keys, verification codes and recovery codes, and enable multi-factor authentication or passkeys where appropriate. Redact usable credentials and unrelated personal information before sending screenshots, logs or request examples to support. We will not ask you to email passwords, complete API keys or recovery codes.

No system can guarantee absolute security. If personal information is leaked, altered or lost, we will take remedial action and make reports or notify affected individuals as required, explaining potential effects and available protective steps.
