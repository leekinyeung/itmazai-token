AI access with a practical workspace

TaoDock is an AI API platform for developers, teams and individuals who want to bring AI into their applications and everyday workflows. It brings model access, API credentials, a playground and usage information together in one dashboard.

Our goal is to make the path from trying an idea to connecting it to an application easier to manage. Explore the available models, configure your integration and review how your requests are being used from the same workspace.

What you can do with TaoDock

  • Connect your application

    Use a shared API entry point to connect supported AI capabilities to your application, scripts or development tools.

  • Explore models and prompts

    Try the available models in the playground, adjust request settings and review responses before building an integration.

  • Manage API access

    Create and manage API keys, review their permissions and apply available expiry, access and usage restrictions.

  • Understand your usage

    Review request history, usage quantities and recorded charges to understand how your applications consume services.

  • Work across content types

    Build with text and supported image, audio or video interfaces according to the capabilities of the selected model.

  • Keep account settings together

    Manage your profile, preferences, account security options and wallet information through the dashboard.

Available models, interfaces and features depend on the current service configuration and your account access. Check the relevant service pages for supported capabilities, limits and pricing.

Built for different ways of working

  • Developers connecting AI to products, tools and automated workflows.
  • Teams organizing application credentials and reviewing usage across their work.
  • Individual users exploring prompts, models and supported creative tasks.

Our approach

Keep integration practical. Bring the controls needed to explore a model, connect an application and manage access into a consistent workspace.

Make usage easier to understand. Give users a place to review requests, usage and charges alongside their account controls.

Encourage thoughtful use. Help users choose capabilities that fit their tasks, review generated results and understand the service rules before relying on an integration.
