TaoDock’s current discounted model rates are **20% of the provider’s official list price**, which means **80% savings** at those rates. We keep costs down in two ways. For closed-source models, we buy lower-priced official subscriptions in volume. For open-source models, we work with compute providers that deploy and run the models themselves.

## Closed-source models powered by official subscriptions

Every closed-source model on TaoDock is the genuine model released by its original provider. We purchase lower-priced official account subscriptions in volume and pass the savings on through lower API rates.

Operating those subscriptions at scale takes work. Our team handles account management, access controls, and request security. You access the models through the TaoDock API, and we handle subscription management and security on our side.

## Open-source models deployed by compute providers

We work with multiple compute providers that deploy the latest open-source models on infrastructure they operate. The cost of serving these models comes largely from GPU compute, deployment, and ongoing operations. With several providers to choose from, we can compare what it costs to serve the same model and offer more competitive rates.
