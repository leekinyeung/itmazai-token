# Wallet and usage

Open [TaoDock Wallet](https://taodock.com/wallet) to check your balance and the funding options available to your account. TaoDock currently displays quota and related amounts in **USD**.

## Check both quota limits {#quota}

1. **Account balance / available plan quota:** check Wallet.
2. **API key quota:** check the settings and remaining quota on [API Keys](https://taodock.com/keys).

A request can fail when the key's quota is exhausted even if the account has a balance. Unlimited quota on a key does not provide unlimited account usage.

## Obtain quota {#funds}

Use the top-up, redemption, or plan options offered in Wallet. Before submitting, check the payment amount, resulting quota, and terms shown on the page.

- For a redemption code, submit it through Wallet and check that the balance changes after success.
- For an online payment, review the order first and follow the payment steps. If payment completed but quota has not arrived, check the order status before making repeated payments for the same issue.
- For a plan, check its validity, supported models and groups, and quota rules. Purchasable plans depend on what Wallet currently offers.

Review the current funding options, amounts, model prices, and terms on the service page before submitting.

## Check a request's charge {#usage}

Review pricing in [Model Square](https://taodock.com/pricing) before calling a model. After the call, check its record in [Common Logs](https://taodock.com/usage-logs/common) or [Task Logs](https://taodock.com/usage-logs/task).

Charges may vary by model, group, input and output usage, and billing method. Token-priced conversations and image- or task-priced services cannot be compared solely by request count.

## Questions about charges or funding {#questions}

Prepare the time, order or request ID, model name, amount, and error details, then use the support contact offered on TaoDock. Do not share passwords, email verification codes, or complete API keys.

A client error alone does not prove that a request was never executed. Check the TaoDock record and status before deciding to submit it again.
